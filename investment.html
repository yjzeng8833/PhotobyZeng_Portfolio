What you can expect

A pre-session consultation

We'll connect beforehand so I can understand your family, your space, and what moments matter most to you. No surprises on the day.

Fine-edited digital images

Every delivered photo is carefully edited in my signature warm, timeless style. These are not quick batch edits — each image is given individual attention.

Private online gallery

Your images are delivered via a beautiful private gallery within 3–4 weeks. Download anytime, share with family, print from anywhere.

Your images are yours to print, frame, and share with family — forever. I'll also provide print recommendations so your images look stunning on the wall.
